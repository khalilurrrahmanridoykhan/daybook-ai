You can build your own personal AI — and you can start with my code.

Imagine an assistant that remembers the context you share, helps manage your tasks, and reminds you when important work is still unfinished.

Your research deadline. Your daily routine. Even that thing your wife asked you to do. 🙂

That is what inspired DayBook AI. I built it for my own daily life, and I’m sharing the code so you can adapt it to yours.

The codebase brings together:
• Persistent conversation memory
• Tasks, notes, and budget tools
• Calendar integration and reminders
• Voice interaction and morning briefings
• An open-weight model running through Ollama on your own server

You don’t need to train a model from scratch. You can build around an existing model, connect your own tools, and shape the assistant around your needs.

Self-hosting gives you more control over AI processing. It also means taking responsibility for security, backups, and the external services you connect.

Working across public health, data systems, and AI has made me value tools that fit real workflows. This is my invitation to experiment with that idea in your own life.

The project takes some technical setup, and it’s still evolving. Fork it, explore the README, adapt the integrations, and improve it.
